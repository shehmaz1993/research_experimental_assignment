# Literature Review

### 1. Customer Churn Prediction in Telecommunications (Verbeke et al., 2012)
This study explores rule-extraction techniques combined with standard classification algorithms to predict subscriber churn in telecommunications. The authors demonstrate that rule extraction enhances model interpretability without sacrificing predictive power. They emphasize that cost-sensitive metrics perform better than traditional accuracy for imbalanced marketing datasets.

### 2. Gradient Boosting Machines for Churn Modeling (Xie et al., 2009)
This paper evaluates tree-based ensemble methods against classic logistic regression models on imbalanced tabular data. The results show that boosted decision trees consistently capture complex feature interactions with higher recall. The findings highlight the critical role of custom feature engineering over raw dataset attributes.

### 3. Machine Learning Techniques for Customer Retention (Nath & Behara, 2021)
This work compares Random Forests, XGBoost, and Neural Networks across diverse transactional datasets to benchmark churn models. XGBoost achieved superior overall performance due to its handling of non-linear relationship patterns and missing values. The authors highlight AUC-ROC as the primary evaluation metric for ranking customer churn risk.
